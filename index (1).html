Tracking enquiries all the way to admissions for ICME India

A medical exam coaching institute was spending on ads but couldn't tell which channels produced students, because most admissions closed over the phone. I built the tracking that connected ad clicks to real enquiries and contact-centre admissions, then used it to run Google and Meta campaigns.

How a click became a measured admission

Two journeys needed tracking. Online actions went through Tag Manager into GA4 and back to Google Ads. Phone admissions, which the website never saw, went to Meta through the Conversions API.

The problem: ads were running, but admissions were invisible

ICME India in Thiruvananthapuram trains doctors for PLAB, AMC, NZREX, USMLE, NEET PG, FMGE, MRCP and MRCS, and runs subject training for MBBS students.

Most students enrolled after a phone call, so website tracking alone missed most conversions. The Meta Pixel couldn't see phone admissions, and enquiries ran through an externally hosted Google Form.

What ICME couldn't answer

  • Which channels produced genuine enquiries
  • Which courses attracted the most interest
  • Whether visitors preferred forms, calls or social media
  • Where visitors dropped off before enquiring
  • How ad spend translated into admissions

Tracking built around enquiries, not page views

Event tracking in GTM and GA4

Set up 10+ enquiry events: consultation and contact form submissions, phone call clicks, email link clicks, Instagram clicks and other contact actions. Each was tested before use.

Google Ads conversion import

Imported the main lead conversions from GA4 into Google Ads, so campaigns optimized toward real enquiries instead of clicks.

UTM standards

Standardized UTM parameters across every campaign for consistent attribution in GA4.

Behaviour analysis

Used Microsoft Clarity recordings and heatmaps to see where visitors hit friction on course pages. Added Google Site Kit so ICME's team could check traffic inside WordPress.

Google Ads tuned to the admission cycle

Competitors were outranking ICME on high-intent searches, and budget was spread evenly regardless of when students were enrolling.

  • Ran campaigns optimized for qualified enquiries, not traffic
  • Sent ad traffic to the relevant course page, not the homepage
  • Planned budgets around ICME's admission windows, spending more when demand peaked

Getting phone admissions back into Meta

Many students clicked a Facebook or Instagram ad, visited the site, then enrolled by phone. Ads Manager never saw those admissions, so Meta under-reported results and optimized on incomplete data.

I set up the Meta Conversions API to send contact-centre admissions back to Meta, rolled out in phases to protect data quality:

  1. Connected CAPI and started with a small share of daily call-centre data
  2. Monitored data quality for the first two weeks and expanded gradually
  3. Scaled up to cover most online and call-centre admissions

A website built to earn trust and make enquiring easy

Audience research

Studied competing medical education sites and surveyed 30 MBBS students, practising doctors and parents from 5 medical colleges in Kerala. Respondents linked the look of Indian government websites with credibility, so I used a formal, government-inspired design made more modern.

WordPress redesign

Redesigned and restructured ICME's existing WordPress site so staff could update courses and batch dates without a developer, with a dedicated page for each exam.

Enquiry paths

Added Enquiry/Registration buttons to every course card, used Google Forms for low-cost lead capture, and added a WhatsApp button.

Trust signals

Featured student exam results, the chief instructor's credentials, a "Why ICME" section and both office addresses with a map, and treated the Google Business Profile as a key touchpoint.

My engagement ran from March to May 2025. ICME then hired an in-house digital marketing specialist, and their team has managed icmeindia.com and its tracking since, so the live site may differ from what's described here.
